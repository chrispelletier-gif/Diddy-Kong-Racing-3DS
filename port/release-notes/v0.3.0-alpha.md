**Performance Part Two - Electric Boogaloo: the renderer update.** The third alpha of Diddy Kong Racing for Nintendo 3DS, an unofficial, native port built from the community decompilation of the N64 game. The game no longer translates N64 graphics commands while it plays; its drawing code now talks to the 3DS graphics chip (PICA200) directly. That means less work for the CPU on every model and less for the GPU on New 3DS. It's still an early alpha.

**No game files are included.** You need your own Diddy Kong Racing ROM, **US version 1.0** (SHA1 `0cb115d8716dbbc2922fda38e533b9fe63bb9670`).

## Installing
1. Copy your ROM to `sdmc:/3ds/dkr/baserom.z64`.
2. Either copy `dkr.3dsx` to `sdmc:/3ds/` and start it from the Homebrew Launcher, or install `dkr.cia` with FBI to get a HOME menu icon.
3. For sound, the DSP firmware dump `sdmc:/3ds/dspfirm.cdc` is needed (made with DSP1), as for other homebrew.

**FBI users:** open FBI > Remote Install > Scan QR Code and scan the QR image below, or use Remote Install > Download from URL and enter

## Highlights
- **A new native renderer** on both consoles: draws are grouped by material, the track is drawn in far fewer draws, and unseen geometry is thrown out early.
- **Original 3DS, 3DS XL, 2DS: a locked 30 fps.** 100% of frames at 30 fps over 200 races, including Whale Bay (0.2: 82%). Worst frame down from 71 to 51 ms.
- **New 3DS and 3DS XL: 60 fps with full 4x anti-aliasing** on every track, with 1.4 ms less renderer CPU a frame and a worst frame of 33 ms (0.2: 49 ms).
- **60 fps now plays like 30 fps and the N64**: air turns, planes, hovercraft, water and Tricky's tunnel all fixed.
- **Hubs show their own map** on the bottom screen, and the Egg Collector panel is roomier.
- **Fixes**: doubled menu music, the grey box when resuming from the HOME menu, tilt steering, saves.

## What's new
- **Native renderer, both consoles:** models, track, sprites, menus and text are drawn natively in batches; the scheduler, audio, music, ROM reads and input are native 3DS code. Frames were checked identical to the old renderer on the PC test build.
- **Original 3DS:** more vertex work on the second core, kart models simplified at a distance, a screen-refresh fix that cut late frames, lost wake-ups fixed.
- **New 3DS:** screen clear as a memory fill, frame buffers in video memory, invisible fades skipped. GPU drawing is down to about 9 ms a frame.
- **60 fps fixes:** air turns and ground sliding, planes, hovercraft and cars, water, scenery and fish, a kart stuck on one or two wheels, bosses, and AI karts turning too sharply.

## Bug fixes and smaller changes
- Doubled menu music after leaving track select (#518).
- New 3DS: grey box when leaving for or resuming from the HOME menu (#519).
- Tricky's tunnel: no extra WRONG WAY after the shortcut (#397).
- Tilt steering did nothing in player builds.
- Widescreen: edge colour of the post-race replay (#510).
- Saves: a failed save could lose the whole file, failed timed saves now retry, and the newest backups show in the list.
- Bottom screen: each hub's own map (#499), a roomier Egg Collector panel (#394), cut-off Settings text.
- New 3DS: about 1 ms of GPU time per frame won back (#509).
- A pre-release code review fixed 40 issues, including track draw order, stereo 3D, texture eviction and the HOME menu.

## Known issues
- New 3DS: one load frame of about 150 to 170 ms at each race start (original 3DS: about 0.7 s).
- Hovercraft at 60 fps is 1 to 3% off the 30 fps feel in long turns.
- Original 3DS: other karts switch to simpler models at a distance.
- Track select can glitch when moving the cursor, and a blue box is still being looked into.
- Adventure mode has been played on a New 3DS as far as Dino Domain and Sherbet Island fully cleared and Snowflake Mountain 6 of 8 (no key or amulet piece yet). Later realms and the original 3DS haven't been played end to end. Back up your saves (Settings > Saves) before trying a new build.
- One player only; multiplayer is hidden. US 1.0 ROM only.

## Upgrading from 0.2.0-alpha
Install the new `dkr.cia` or `dkr.3dsx` over the old one; the ROM and saves (`dkr.eep`, `dkr.mpk`) stay where they are. Backing up from Settings > Saves first is a good idea.

## Reporting a problem
After a crash the game saves `last_crash.txt` and `last_log.txt` in `sdmc:/3ds/dkr/`. Open an issue with what you were doing, your console model and this version number, and attach them. Debug builds can post a bug report straight from the game.
